# DesignatedDriver Roadmap

This roadmap describes direction, not guaranteed dates.

## Shipped

- customer rescue-request and vehicle flows
- driver job, arrival, inspection, active-drive, and completion flows
- admin operations and gear-verification surfaces
- Supabase Auth/Postgres/RLS/Storage foundation
- Chase Car and Solo-Scoot operating models
- trip-state, dispatch, pricing, and evidence-upload prototypes
- extensive demo/test preparation documentation

## Improving

- repository/documentation consolidation
- end-to-end role-flow regression coverage
- dispatch/state-machine hardening
- mobile accessibility and error recovery
- production-safe environment setup

## Exploring

- clearer dispatcher tooling
- operational analytics
- notification/communication workflows
- deployment architecture for a controlled pilot

## Product boundary

This remains a prototype until dispatch, insurance/operational requirements, safety procedures, and production data handling are validated outside the codebase.
